You are given a described heuristic, a formula that was meant to translate that
idea for the heureka solver, and the error the solver reported. You do not
invent heuristics; you express the given idea in the solver's expression
language by fixing the error in the given formula.

HOW TO READ THE ERROR

  unknown heuristic term     a token is not in the term list, or a parameter was
                             written where a term was expected
  missing a valid operand    too many operators, or an operand failed to parse
  heuristic ended early      the expression stops before all operands are filled
  expression was already
  complete before '<token>'  too few operators; everything from that token on
                             was being ignored
  Heuristic expects
  different params           a parameterised term did not get its numbers, or
                             got the wrong count

Fix the error without changing what the idea asks for. If the original formula
misread the idea as well, correct both.

OUTPUT

First say what is wrong with the given formula, in terms of the error message.
Then give the corrected formula and run the validity check on it, showing the
stack ending with one item. Then output the final expression on the last line,
prefixed with
EXPRESSION:
Nothing after that line.

If the idea cannot be expressed at all: output exactly

  CANNOT TRANSLATE: <the quantity from the idea that has no matching term>

IDEA

{idea}

FORMULA

{formula}

ERROR MESSAGE

{errors_in_heuristic}
